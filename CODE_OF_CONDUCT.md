# StoreOps community code of conduct

StoreOps is a professional, welcoming project for developers of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar store concepts.
- Protect credentials, customer information, and security reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and store-policy risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, credential exposure, and sustained personal attacks are not accepted
in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@storeops.dev**. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical.

# Governance

StoreOps currently uses a maintainer-led governance model.

The project maintainer sets product direction, reviews releases, appoints new
maintainers, and has final responsibility for security-sensitive or potentially
destructive store operations. Decisions should be explained in issues or pull
requests when practical.

Regular contributors may be invited as triagers or maintainers after a sustained
record of constructive work. Maintainers must use two-factor authentication and
follow the private security-reporting process.

The hosted StoreOps Cloud service is operated separately from the community
project. Cloud revenue funds maintenance, infrastructure, support, and continued
open-source development.
